# Core Framework

# Copywriting Slogan Full-Dimension Practical Appraisal System

This framework has five core dimensions and one meltdown mechanism. When judging or writing a line of commercial copy, pass it through these five gates in order.

## Dimension 1: Strategic Anchoring And Game Layer

Core question: Who are you fighting? Where is the battlefield?

This layer solves the right to survive.

### 1. Exclusionary Verdict And Counter-Common-Sense Positioning

Original theory: USP.

Practical upgrade:

- Attack the opponent's underlying logic. Do not merely say you are better. Declare the opponent's approach invalid.
- Example: Tadpole says "No noise filtering, just physics." It is not saying the algorithm is better. It says algorithm is the wrong path and physics is the right path.
- Master move: Find the industry's standard answer, such as noise reduction algorithms or replacement, then stand on the opposite side, such as physical isolation or repair.
- Test: Cover the logo. If a competitor can also use the sentence, delete and rewrite.

### 2. Reference Frame Shifting

Original theory: positioning.

Practical upgrade:

- Stretch the time or space axis. When competitors compare the present, compare history. When competitors compare parameters, compare the world.
- Example: Nike says "Be the fastest runner in history." The competitor changes from the runner in the next lane to human history.
- Master move: Do not try to be the best in the category. Define the category.

### 3. Identity And The Altar Of Values

Original theory: brand archetype.

Practical upgrade:

- Self-empowerment. Elevate consumption into moral or identity confirmation.
- Example: L'Oreal says "Because I'm worth it." It turns spending money into self-reward. Nike says "Sacrificing everything." It turns sport into faith.
- Master move: Give the user a noble reason to buy with confidence, even when the product is expensive.

## Dimension 2: Cognitive Physics And Noise Reduction Layer

Core question: How many calories does the brain need to process this sentence?

This layer solves entry into the mind.

### 1. Extreme Entropy Reduction And Concrete Nail

Original theory: information entropy and signal-to-noise ratio.

Practical upgrade:

- Reject abstract big words. Avoid Possibility, Future, Solutions, Quality.
- Implant sensory nails. Use at least one word that can be seen, heard, or touched.
- Example: Do not say "good endurance." Say "Amsterdam to Brussels." Do not say "quiet." Say "Shhhh."
- Master move: Replace adjectives such as "small" with nouns such as "pocket" or verbs such as "fit."

### 2. Visual Rhetoric And Typographic Synesthesia

Original theory: cognitive fluency.

Practical upgrade:

- Text is image. Text is not only read. It is seen.
- Example: Apple's "Brilllllliant" stretches letters. Rolaids' "S-p-e-l-l-s Relief" makes spelling itself the action.
- Master move: Look at the core word and ask whether it can change color, stretch, become bold, or be cut off so the letterform itself carries the meaning.

## Dimension 3: Human Backdoor And Desire Layer

Core question: Which primitive button are you pressing?

This layer solves impulse.

### 1. Fear, Greed, And Cognitive Dissonance Stitching

Original theory: reptilian brain mechanism.

Practical upgrade:

- Create dissonance and then stitch it. First provoke the contradiction, such as wanting to buy but being afraid, then give the user a step down.
- Example: ADU's "Way small. Yay big." It is small, but the pleasure is big. Apple's "Think outside the outlet" defines the power outlet as bondage and buying the Mac as freedom.
- Master move: Find a hidden desire, such as laziness, vanity, or gluttony, then tell the user this is acceptable, even good.

### 2. Personification And Indirect Praise

Original theory: identity projection.

Practical upgrade:

- Praise through an object or third party. Let a dog, house, old car, neighbor, or owner generate emotion to support the product.
- Example: "Your main home might get jealous." "Ask the man who owns one."
- Master move: If direct boasting feels awkward, create a jealous figure or a witness.

## Dimension 4: Language Engineering And Acoustic Layer

Core question: Does this sentence read like poetry or like an instruction manual?

This layer solves memory.

### 1. Expectation Violation And Idiom Hacking

Original theory: stickiness.

Practical upgrade:

- Build expectation, then break it. Quote half an idiom and alter the other half.
- Example: "From dusk till dawn... and beyond." It builds an all-night expectation, then exceeds it. "Don't just travel. Travel right." It builds travel expectation, then adds moral judgment.
- Master move: Search idioms related to the industry, then change the last two words to create reversal.

### 2. Phonosemantics And Onomatopoeic Impact

Original theory: phonological loop.

Practical upgrade:

- Use sound as metonymy. Let sound replace description.
- Example: "Zoom Zoom" is the sound of speed. "Oops, Whoops, Oh no" is the sound of dropping a phone. "Bite" uses explosive sound to express grip.
- Master move: Read the slogan aloud. If it sounds flat, replace it with words that have explosive force, such as Bang, Boom, Pop, or friction, such as Smooth, Sizzle.

## Dimension 5: Logical Loop And Scene Layer

Core question: Is the logic closed? Is the scene grounded?

This layer solves credibility.

### 1. Meta-Concepts And Logical Mobius Loop

Original theory: this is a high-dimensional practical technique added by this framework.

Practical upgrade:

- Act on itself. Let the action loop back onto the actor.
- Example: "Make the repair tool repairable." "Tools that shape how people make."
- Master move: Ask whether the product has a recursive property. If yes, use it. This creates philosophical depth.

### 2. Headline-Body Interlock

Original theory: funnel thinking.

Practical upgrade:

- The slogan is the hook. The body is the blade. The slogan creates feeling and surprise. The body gives reason and evidence.
- Example: The slogan says "Sandstorm ready." The body gives "8 hours confined storm" and concrete data.
- Master move: Do not let headline and body repeat each other. The headline asks and the body answers. The headline boasts and the body gives evidence.

## Meltdown Mechanism: Death List

Before publishing copy, perform the final check. If any rule is violated, melt it down and delete or rewrite.

### 1. Zombie Word Meltdown

Does it contain Solution, Possibility, Future, Quality, Passion, or Innovation? If yes, replace with concrete verbs or nouns.

### 2. Self-Indulgence Meltdown

Is the subject mostly We or Our? If yes, force it into You or Your, or start with a verb.

### 3. Translation Meltdown

Translate it into another language. If the rhetoric disappears and the result becomes bland nonsense, such as "small but strong," the logical core is weak. Rewrite.

### 4. Replacement Meltdown

Cover the brand name and replace it with a competitor. If it still reads smoothly, it has no exclusivity. Rewrite.

## Final Reminder

Mediocre copy describes the product.

Good copy solves a problem.

Top copy reframes the world.

Do not merely write ads. Write declarations, truths, and sentences that enter the mind like nails.
