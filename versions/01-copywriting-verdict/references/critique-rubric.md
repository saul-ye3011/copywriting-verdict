# Critique Rubric

## Verdict Levels

- Kill
- Rewrite
- Usable
- Strong
- Sharp

## Default Short Critique

Use five lines unless the user asks for depth.

1. Verdict
2. Core defect or core strength
3. Strongest dimension
4. Meltdown item
5. Rewrite direction

## Five-Dimensional Scan

Strategic anchoring  
Does it have an enemy, battlefield, exclusionary verdict, or reference frame shift?

Cognitive physics  
Does it enter the brain through concrete words, sensory hooks, or visual form?

Human desire  
Does it press fear, greed, pride, laziness, status, freedom, belonging, or self-justification?

Language engineering  
Does it have rhythm, sound, surprise, idiom hacking, or repeatability?

Scene logic  
Does it close the loop with proof, scene, recursion, or headline-body cooperation?

Object sense  
Who is being spoken to, what are they already seeing, and what does the sentence cause them to notice, understand, feel, compare, or do next?

## Meltdown

Kill or rewrite if the line contains:

- Solution
- Possibility
- Future
- Quality
- Passion
- Innovation
- We or Our dominance
- Abstract adjective stacking
- Competitor-replaceable claim
- Translation-only cleverness
- Claim without evidence
- No identifiable listener or audience moment
- Visual transcription that duplicates what the viewer can already see
- Report framing such as `the video shows`, `this post presents`, or `a closer look at` with no audience value
- A sentence whose removal costs no meaning, interest, judgment, or action

## Rewrite Rule

Do not rewrite before diagnosing. The rewrite should directly answer the diagnosed defect.
